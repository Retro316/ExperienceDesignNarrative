Chracters Backstories

Xavier "“Ironbound”

Before he was a part of The Order of the Broken Crown Xavier was a child about 19 years old at the time, when he lived in a kingdom named Dravenholt. He was best friends with a girl named Aveline who was 17, with black barided hair and silver eyes. The kingdom they lived in was placed in the mountains, where there is always snow. They played in the snow and "trained" as soilders as they both wanted to be protecters of their kingdom. Bravenholt was one of the strogest kingdoms in the land. That was until on one fateful night when it all came crumbling down. In the middle of the night, the raid bell started to ring and the kingdom was under attack. When Xavier came out of his room he saw both his parents dead in front of men wearing the Dravenholt gaurd's uniforms. In response to seeing his dead parents and he ran back to his room to sneak out through his window avoiding the gaurds that invaded his home. He remember the hidden cave that him and Aveline go to, to escape and watch the stars and bolted there in hopes of seeing her. As he dashed through the burning kingdom and rushing down the path to the cave, he saw blood trails in the snow, with a scared heart he rushed and followed them to find Aveline laying in a pool of blood, with a Dravenhotl gaurd standing wounded from Aveline's last breath. With a rush of adrenaline he pulled out his dagger and rushed at the wounded gaurd jumping on top of him and stabbing him repeatedly until his eyes turned lifeless. He looked back at his dead friend and wept. After he came to his senses he picked Aveline's body and carried her to the hidden cave burying right outside. When he was done he heard footsteps approach and when he turned around he was met with a hooden figure who said "I saw what you did to that gaurd, you are strong and I would like to ask for your help. Kingdom's always fall because of tyrants and I am part of an orginization that stops that. If you join us, you could help save many of lives from following the same fate as your friend here." Xavier was hesitant but he had no one else to turn to. His kingdom fell, his parents dead, and his best friend gone. He agreed but only if he was able to lead his own group of assassins.
