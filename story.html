The Past

A secret orginization called "The Severance" where people come together to forward thier believes on how advance the world for the better by destruction, turf take over, and assassinations. A small 3 man group a part of the orginization named **"The Crescent Rose"** with Meave, Corvin, and Xavier as thier leader are responsable for a part of the assassinations that The Severance is responsable for. This however changed after one a the higher ups of the orginization sent the 3 to assassinate a princess of a kingdom where she would only be escorted by 3 gaurds, another easy mission for the 3. Upon completing this mission, The Crescent Rose would fully become a part of the orginization.

With the mission being underway, the 3 were able to takeout the 3 gaurds escorting the princess with ease. When the 3 entered the carrage escorting her they were hesitant to see a little girl about 11 years out look at the 3 and say "Are you hear to see mommy too?" This broke the 3 and relized what this mission was trully meant to show. Follow orders and ask no questions. They felt betrayed and with reinforcments they wanted to flee but Xavier wanted to stay and protect the child even though the gaurds were for her. As Meave and Corvin surrendered Xavier battle through the gaurds, resulting with one of his arms being cut off and leaving Xavier tired and defeated untimitly leading to The Crescent Rose being Captured.

With The Crescent Rose captured, they were brought to the king of the kingdom. The king asked for thier story of what was asked of them and what thier mission was. Xavier, while kneeling before the king holding his arm that was cut off and wrapped in bandages from the gaurds, told them about thier orginization, what their purpose was, and why they were sent assassinate the king's daughter. He also added that they would not have gone about the mission if they knew that it was a little girl. Corvin stepped in say that when he did his recon before the mission, he knew about the princess's age leading Xavier to lash out at Corvin in front of the king. Meave cut between the two and added herself that they can not return the orginization as they have failed and disapprove of them. With the king hearing the colfict between the gaurding of the princess and the hesitation of knowing her age he was still in a perdicament but though of an idea to show thier loyalty. He proposed that they become "The King's Shadow" where they will be an elite gorup of hidden gaurds learking in the shadows keeping the kingdom safe. The Crescent Rose at that moment was rebranded and under new comand, becoming The King's Shadow.

With this new era for the 3, they were givin task with the watchful eye of the already loyal gaurds of the Kingdom. Corvin was to be placed in room where he is to use his crows to survey the land to find any trouble makers and send the gaurds out to help the civillians. Meave was tasked with medicen making, with the King gifting her a garden that the queen used to use before passing away. Xavier was given the task of roaming the town with the eye of corvin following him. Xavier's task however was on hault and changed soon as his arm was still missing. With the king's permission, and over the course of almost a month, Xavier rested while Meave was allowed to start something to give Xavier his arm back (somewhat). She planted a seed and sung to it for weeks gorwing it with magic. When it was ready she crafted the tree into an arm and fused it with Xavier, giving him a wooden arm. Soon after it was discovered that Whenever the princess was around him it reacted to the situation. When he saw her, the arm bloomed small flowers, and when she was sad, the tree cried sap. With this the King had no doubt that this wasn't just the plant reacting to the surrounding people but Xavier's personal oath to protect the princess allowing Xavier to be her personal guardian.
