The Order of the Broken Crown

Belief: Tyrants always rise from kingdoms and courts

This Order was founded by who everyone calls "Hood." Where he believes that some kingdom's rulers should meet death as they will soon become corrupted via rulers turning into tyratns. Because of this Belief, this Order and orginization was made to assemble common folk and travlers to come together and build a world with no kings and have everyone be equal to live in unity.

The Crescent Rose

An Assassination force that was a part of the "Order of the Broken Crown." This group consist of Xavier, Meave, and Corvin. They were ordered and sworn to help them in order to stop tyrants from rising from kingdom to kingdom. Later on they shall carry out thier last triar mission before they can become fully apart of the Order. This was when they were meet with a target of an 11 year old princess only seconds before the killing blow was to be dealt.
