I have arrived in Ordinis and its exactly what I expected it to be from my intel. Broken homes, ripped cloths, and people scrounging for food. But something that I did not expect was the unity this kingdom still had. There was people trying to help one another. People acting selflessly. I haven't seen this in a while from a kingdom in such ruin but that's what I am here to do. keep it in ruin

A disaster happen today, I rain so heavy that it flooded part of the kingdom. But that did not stop these people. It's like they have seen worse. What happened before I came here. When trying to get more intel on why these people had so much hope he just laughed and said that everyone is too stubborn to die. He may be right.

Today I saw a man named Evander who was a part of the royal guard help a widow with her broken roof after curfew. He didn't care for the punishment. He was one if not the only royal guard who had helped the people and cared not for the law as he saw it's rottenness. I have seen kingdoms stronger than this fall in days. Yet this one refuses to kneel

The other Harbinger wanted me to help him stop this upcoming rebellion by spreading lies and dwindle their hope, I will do no such thing. I've seen Kingdoms stronger than this fall in days, yet this place refuses to kneel, a light has sparked inside me, I must help

The rebellion quietly grows louder, not against the throne but the rot around it. I stitched a boy up and instead of saying thank you he apologized for bleeding on my cloths. I laughed so hard I nearly cried. When I asked why this young boy was helping in such a harsh rebellion he just smiled and said "someone has to make tomorrow kinder "What terrifying faith. What beautiful foolishness. I once believed power was the only thing capable of changing the world. Now I am no longer certain.

I was caught by Evander but not in trouble by him, He caught me stuck on the roof top. I've never been so caught off guard, there's something about this man that interreges me. When he helped me, we talked and he asked why I was here and stayed. I didn't tell him the whole truth of why but instead just told him that every day I see the people and him talk about a brighter future I actually start to believe in one, I fear that more than any monster.

The rebellion has won and the people elected Evander as the new king for his kindness and bravery to the townspeople. You should have seen his face. He looked less like a ruler and more like a man terrified of failing those who believed in him. Good. Fear will keep him human. But even through his fear, he asked me to stand beside him. As his queen. It still feels like a borrowed title as I have worn many masks in my life. This one I truly wish to be mine.

Today we adopted a child, not a human, or elf, and tiefling. A boy wondering in the kingdom with no family or friends. His name, Cullum. He tried stealing Evander's rings during our first meet. Evander tried not to notice until the boy proudly announced he had succeeded. I've never seen Evander laugh so hard. The court protested, of course. I silenced them with a single sentence: "There is nothing wrong with giving someone would was delt a bad hand a second chance". Now Cullum sleeps in his own bed, in his own room with silverware he insist he did not take. I think this may be the happiest I have ever been.

She looks just like me, Lily. I don't think I've ever seen Evander hold onto something or someone for that matter so tightly in his life. Cullum declared himself her protector as he realized what a family we were coming to be. but I fear, even now while I hold my daughter, I feel like the distant pull of something old is coming back. I know this feeling all too well.

I was right, something is coming. They have returned or perhaps they never truly left. I do not know who they are but I can only guess that Harbinger is back. I do not know his exact identity but I do know his tactics. I have hidden a relic within this kingdom that can help fight against it. He uses fear to control others so if the day does come, this relic will help you.

If you are reading this, then I did not return, they are here, and you may need the relic I spoke of. I original started this journal to help me keep this kingdom in ruin and now I use it for my own pleasure and not to help this kingdom stay in the light. I've hoped that I would be able to escape my past life but perhaps I was never truly allowed to. The raiders have started to attack the kingdom and instead of fighting alongside it, I've decided to sacrifice myself. I believe this is for the best. I wish I could have watch Lily grow. I wish I could have watch Cullum grow to be a great prince. and I wish I could have grown old with my love Evander. But some debts demand payment. If sacrifice is the price to keep Ordinis alive, then I hope my name in history keeps it that way. So long as my children live long enough to speak it.
