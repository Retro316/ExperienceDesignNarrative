Rose (The Queen)

Like a rose she was beautiful but prickly. She was a "robinhood" in the kingdom and wanted to help the commoners and take down the corrupt Royals. During one of her mission, going undercover, she meet the King. She learned that he was doing his best to protect the people but didn't know how to fix it. While when she did some more recon she found the corruption in the court and reported it back to the King. Down the line, they became partners, slowly fixing the Kingdom into being more free while trying to have the royals give the commoners more freedom. Thier partner ship soon lead to them falling in love and birthing Cealum & Lily.

Death/Disappearance

One night, the kingdom was raided without warning. No one knew who these raiders could be. The kingdom was not activly fighting anyone nor were the raider's wearing any symbols to show where they originated from. when both the king and queen were woken up the Evander was ready to fight and defend his kingdom but his wife stopped him telling him to protect thier children. When He entered the childrens bedroom to protect them, he soon relized that Rose was missing. He search all over the castle, even asking gaurds if they have seen her. Minutes later the sound of screaming and explosions stopped and all was quiet. The raiders left. Reports for citizens say that they saw a red hooded person provoke the raiders into chasing them into the woods. The King knew only two things in that moment. They were after the red hooded figure and that he may never see his wife again.
