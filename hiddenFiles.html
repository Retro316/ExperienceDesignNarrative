Rose (The Queen)

Like a rose she was beautiful but prickly. She was a "robinhood" in the kingdom and wanted to help the commoners and take down the corrupt Royals. During one of her mission, going undercover, she meet the King. She learned that he was doing his best to protect the people but didn't know how to fix it. While when she did some more recon she found the corruption in the court and reported it back to the King. Down the line, they became partners, slowly fixing the Kingdom into being more free while trying to have the royals give the commoners more freedom. Thier partner ship soon lead to them falling in love and birthing Lily...
