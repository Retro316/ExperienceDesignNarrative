The prophecy (in-depth)

“The Princess who walks between shadow and steel shall wear a crown unseen, and kings will kneel where she stands.”

They think: She will become a tyrant / conqueror “Shadow” = evil, assassins, darkness The truth: “Shadow and steel” refers to her alliance with the ex-assassins “crown unseen” means she becomes a leader without a throne (maybe leading a rebellion or hidden network) “Kings kneel” = she earns loyalty, not domination
